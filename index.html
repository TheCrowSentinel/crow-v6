Buyer-led. Human-mediated.

The buyer approves a normal release. Crow's intended mediator holds the release secret and could technically release to the agreed seller. This model depends on trusting Crow.

Expiry means a refund path.

A fixed ledger deadline cannot be paused by a dispute. After expiry, a cancellation transaction is needed to return the escrow to the buyer. Refunds are not automatic.

05 / THE DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I use real XRP on this site?+

No. Crow v6 is a complete interactive website and local escrow demo. It does not connect a wallet, create ledger escrows, or move XRP. The amounts and trades are illustrative.

Who controls a release?+

In the intended model, you approve normal release as the buyer and sign with Xaman. Crow keeps a unique release secret for each escrow and mediates disputes. Possession of that secret gives Crow technical release authority before expiry. XRPL does not enforce buyer and mediator voting.

What happens when a delivery is disputed?+

Normal release stops while a human reviews the agreement and evidence. A seller-favorable decision can authorize release before expiry. A buyer-favorable decision withholds release and waits for expiry so the escrow can be cancelled. A dispute cannot extend the ledger deadline. This demo lets you explore those outcomes yourself.

Does the inspection timer release funds automatically?+

No. The inspection period is a reminder to review the goods or work. Normal release still needs your explicit approval, and inspection cannot extend the fixed escrow expiry.

How do construction milestones work?+

Treat each milestone as its own escrow with a clearly scoped outcome. The demo can represent one milestone per trade. It does not split an existing ledger escrow into partial releases.

Where is my demo data saved?+

Trade labels, sample amounts, and demo activity are saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Export creates a local JSON backup; Reset demo replaces local trades with the original samples. Use fictional details and never enter wallet secrets or personal information.
